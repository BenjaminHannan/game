# Open unknowns

Running ledger of unresolved design questions. Resolve a milestone's entries with the owner *before* building that milestone; move answered ones to the bottom with their decision.

## Open

- **First five minutes** — when the owner pictures playing: laying roads and painting zones from nothing, or watching a city already in motion? (Shapes onboarding and what ships next.)
- **Traffic model** — individual agents pathing per-vehicle (CS2-like, expensive) vs. statistical flow along edges (cheap, less alive). Architecture-changing; must be answered before the traffic milestone.
- **Art style** — low-poly flat-shaded vs. textured realism. Prototype 2–3 building-style variants and let the owner pick from screenshots.
- **City scale target** — cozy town (hundreds of buildings) vs. sprawl (thousands); sets performance budgets for meshes and sim tick.
- **Zoning feel** — CS2-style painted zone grids along roads, or per-lot placement?
- **Final name** — Metropolis is a working title.

## Decided

- **Project identity** — city-builder inspired by Cities: Skylines II, not an idle game (owner, 2026-08-08). Original branding only; no trademarked naming.
- **Road MVP** — straight segments with node snapping first; curves later (roads research doc, 2026-08-08).
